The question your product can't answer

Your customers ask your software one deceptively simple thing: is this compliant? Leave and wage law is fifty-plus jurisdictions of moving targets — minimum wages that reindex every year, brand-new paid-leave programs, overtime and exemption rules that change by city. Today you either staff a rules team, hard-code answers that quietly go stale, or let an LLM guess. All three are liabilities.

What you embed
  • Every conclusion cites its statute — a justification tree, not a black box your customers can't defend.
  • Effective-dated — "the law as of any date," so back-pay and audits reconstruct exactly what applied, and when.
  • Discretion is flagged, never faked — open-textured questions come back for a human; coverage gaps are reported, never hidden.
  • Two call styles — REST for your backend, MCP for your AI features.

Why embed, not build.

You don't want to be in the employment-law-maintenance business. Encoding the law once is easy; keeping it current across every jurisdiction, forever, is a team you'd rather not run. That maintenance — an LLM-drafted, regression-gated, counsel-reviewed amendment pipeline — is what you're actually buying.

For AI copilots.

Point your assistant at the MCP server and it calls a verified oracle instead of hallucinating leave law — returning citations your users, and their lawyers, can check. LLM at the edges, verified engine at the core.
