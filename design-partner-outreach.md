# OpenLeave — Design-Partner Outreach

Cold-outbound copy for reaching the two embed audiences: **platforms** (payroll / HRIS / EOR /
leave-management) and **AI HR copilots**. Pairs with the pitch in
[`design-partner-onepager.pdf`](design-partner-onepager.pdf).

Keep it short, lead with their problem, make one soft ask. Swap `{…}` placeholders and add a
genuine one-line personalization up top (something specific about *their* product) — that first line
matters more than anything below it.

## Notes on using these

- **Personalize line one, always.** The generic body works; a canned first line kills it. Reference
  something real about their product or a recent launch.
- **Stay honest — it's the tone that converts here.** OpenLeave is a working *prototype*; statutory
  figures are being verified by counsel now. Say so if asked; leading with "design partner" already
  sets that expectation. Don't claim production-ready or legal-advice.
- **One ask, low friction.** "20 minutes" beats "let's schedule a demo." No attachments on the first
  touch — a single link.
- **Segment, don't blast.** Send A to platforms and B to copilots; a wrong-audience send is obvious.
- **Volume compliance.** If you send at any scale, follow CAN-SPAM: a truthful subject, a real
  physical mailing address, and a working opt-out. Warm intros beat cold sends every time — use them
  where you have them.
