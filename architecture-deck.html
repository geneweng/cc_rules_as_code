OpenLeave — ArchitectureThe Thesis
§ 1The Thesis
Every decision that follows falls out of one commitment.

LLM at the edges, engine at the core.

The LLM belongs at the edges; a verified rules engine belongs at the core. the survey's organizing thesis, made concrete

Language models draft amendments and hold the conversation. They never decide the law. Taking that seriously forces four properties:

  • The core is deterministic — the same facts and date always yield the same answer.
  • Every conclusion carries its citation — nothing is asserted without a source.
  • Genuine discretion is surfaced, not guessed — open questions come back flagged.
  • The engine reports the limits of its own coverage instead of bluffing.

OPENLEAVE — ARCHITECTURE3
OpenLeave — ArchitectureParameters
§ 3Parameters
One mechanism buys "the law as of any date" — and it is what the amendment pipeline maintains. openleave/parameters.json

Numbers stay data, on a timeline.

# parameters.json — each key is an effective-dated [date, value] series "minwage.CA": [["2025-01-01", 16.50], ["2026-01-01", 16.90]] parameters.get("minwage.CA", as_of=date(2025, 6, 1)) -> 16.50 parameters.get("minwage.CA", as_of=date(2026, 6, 1)) -> 16.90

A wage that clears California's 2025 floor fails its 2026 floor. Reconstructing a past determination for an audit is just passing an earlier as_of. SAWW bumps, benefit caps, program launch dates, and the vacated-then-restored federal exempt threshold are all just points on a timeline.

OpenLeave — ArchitectureVocabulary
§ 4Vocabulary
A leave finding and a wage finding are the same kind of object. openleave/engine.py

Everything is a cited Finding.

Finding( key="serious_health_condition", description="The condition is a 'serious health condition'", met=None, # -> a human must decide (never guessed) citation=Citation("29 C.F.R. § 825.113"), )

met=None is load-bearing: it means the question is open-textured and a human must decide it. resolve_eligibility propagates that None through — discretion is never silently collapsed to a boolean. The same rule flags an exemption's duties test.

OpenLeave — ArchitectureInvariants
§ 5Invariants
The heart of the design: each rule exists to prevent a specific way a rules-oracle lies.

Built to refuse the easy lie.

InvariantFailure it prevents
Every conclusion cites its source Unverifiable or hallucinated law
Discretion is flagged, never compiled (met = null) Auto-answering open-textured questions — "laundered interpretation"
Coverage is reported, never assumed Silent under-coverage — a partial answer read as complete
Outside the encoded range ≠ no entitlement Reading "we lack the historical rate" as "no benefit"
Enacted ≠ in force (e.g. MD FAMLI, 2028) Fabricating a benefit that cannot be claimed yet
Law as of any date Answering a past event with today's numbers

The guarantees   Parameter updates are machine-appliable; structural changes are flagged requires_human_encoding and never auto-applied. A diff that rewrites an in-force historical value breaks pinned determinations and is rejected. Nothing ships without passing tests and a recorded sign-off.
OPENLEAVE — ARCHITECTURE9
OpenLeave — ArchitectureTrust
§ 9Trust
The one thing standing between an impressive prototype and reliance. openleave/references.json

The trust boundary, drift-guarded.

Every statutory value here is web-researched and unverified by counsel — the load-bearing caveat. The manifest is the worksheet for closing it.

  • Maps every parameter to a plain-English meaning, its governing statute, and the agency page to check it against — with per-jurisdiction sign-off fields.
  • A test asserts it documents exactly the encoded parameter set — so a rate cannot be added without a citation, and the build fails if one is.
  • No jurisdiction can be marked verified without a named reviewer and date.
  • The MCP lookup tool returns each value's source and verification status, so a consumer always knows a figure is still pending review.
OPENLEAVE — ARCHITECTURE10
OpenLeave — ArchitectureEdges & Extension
§ 10Edges & Extension
The seams are guarded so you cannot add law without documenting its source.

Thin edges, guarded seams.

The surfaces

All three — MCP server (4 tools), REST API + browser checkers, and the sample agent — call the same two core functions and add nothing to the reasoning. The agent is the only place a model answers a user, and even there it is forbidden to answer substantive questions except through the tools.

Where to extend

Add a leave regime, a wage topic, or a jurisdiction — the recurring pattern is that the manifest drift-guard and the tests force you to document its source. That is the architecture enforcing its own central promise: no law without a citation.
